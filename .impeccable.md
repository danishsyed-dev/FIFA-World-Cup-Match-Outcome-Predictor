## Design Context

### Users
- **Who**: Football fans, sports analysts, data science enthusiasts, and amateur bettors.
- **Context**: Checking match predictions before a fixture, investigating team form, comparing Elo ratings, or testing batch predictions.
- **Job to be Done**: Quickly get a reliable match outcome prediction and understand the underlying reasons (Elo, form, H2H) through high-clarity stats.
- **Emotions**:evoke analytical confidence, tactical excitement, and statistical trust.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice/Tone**: Tactical, Sleek, Precision.
- **Feel**: Like a high-end sports analytics room or tactical playbook (e.g., *The Athletic* meets modern sports telemetry).
- **Anti-References**: Avoid generic "AI templates" (neon cyan-to-purple gradients, generic glassmorphism cards, glowing borders, plain Bootstrap/Streamlit gray look).

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Theme**: Sleek Tactical Slate & Pitch Green (Dark Mode). Neutral colors tinted with dark, rich olive/forest hues (`oklch(0.18 0.02 140)`).
- **Typography**: Display/Metrics in a geometric, clean font (e.g., "Outfit" or "Lexend Deca") for readability of numbers. UI copy in "Inter" or "Geist Sans".
- **Color Palette**:
  - Backgrounds: Deep Slate-Forest (`#0f1716` or `oklch(0.15 0.015 150)`)
  - Accent / Highlights: Tactical Gold (`#d4af37` or `#e5c158`) and Pitch Green (`#10b981`)
  - Home Team Accent: Cool Azure (`#38bdf8`)
  - Away Team Accent: Crimson Red (`#f87171`)
  - Neutral / Text: High-contrast off-white (`#f8fafc`) and muted olive-gray (`#94a3b8`) for secondary labels.
- **Visual Elements**: Clean borders (`1px solid #2d3732`), subtle elevation, asymmetric grid hierarchy, clear typographic weights, and no unnecessary card nesting.

### Design Principles
1. **Precision & Density**: Display data with clean borders, clear labels, and compact, tactical visual rhythm. Avoid large, empty spaces or bloated cards.
2. **Context-Driven Color**: Colors must convey meaning (e.g., Team A colors, Draw/Warning gold, Team B colors). Avoid arbitrary decorative rainbows.
3. **Structured Hierarchy**: Ensure the main prediction stands out instantly, while supporting analytical statistics are grouped logically below it.
4. **Interactive Responsiveness**: Standard inputs should feel responsive, with hover effects and clear focus indicators.
